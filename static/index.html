What This Module Does
Most stock moves are just following the overall market (QQQ/SPY) or tech sector. This cockpit strips away that noise so you can see if the stock is actually moving on its own. When a stock gets unfairly hammered or overpumped compared to its peers, the meter spikes into the alert zone—telling you it's stretched like a rubber band and ready to snap back.
Continuously isolates clean idiosyncratic price dislocations by subtracting real-time market index drift (e.g. QQQ) and sector factor beta (e.g. IGV) via an adaptive dual-factor Kalman Filter. Residual innovation errors ($\epsilon_t$) are normalized into an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck standardized Z-score.
How to Profit & Trade It
Go Long on Green (Oversold), Go Short on Red (Overbought). When the meter hits Green (Z ≤ -σ*), enter a Long Asset position in underlying shares because the stock is statistically primed to mean-revert back upward. When it hits Red (Z ≥ +σ*), enter a Short Asset position because the stock is overstretched. Exit the trade after reaching the calibrated holding horizon sessions.
Formulates an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck boundary crossing policy: execute long ATM Call when normalized 1-day innovation shock $Z_t \le -\sigma^*$ ($E[\Delta X_{t+h} | Z_t \le -\sigma^*] >0$) and long ATM Put when $Z_t \ge +\sigma^*$. Position sizing is governed by calibrated half-life $\tau = \ln(2)/\kappa$, liquidating upon ribbon convergence $|Z_{t+k}| \le 0.5\sigma$ or mandatory expiration at $H^*$ sessions.

What This Module Does
This is your visual trade execution chart. It shows you the stock price with clear green buy arrows (oversold dip ready to bounce) and red sell arrows (overbought rally ready to drop). It also paints the background green or red when the stock enters a strong trend, warning you to never fight a powerful macro trend.
Maps underlying raw daily close price candles in 1:1 temporal alignment with discrete Kalman innovation triggers (▲ Bullish Green / ▼ Bearish Red) and continuous CUSUM cumulative drift regime-shift shading.
How to Profit & Trade It
Trade with the background, not against it. If you see a green buy arrow and the background is neutral, buy the call. But if the background turns solid Red (Bearish Drift), avoid buying calls even if an arrow appears—the stock is in a macro markdown. Use the zoom slider to inspect how past arrows caught major swing bottoms and tops.
Employs a Page's CUSUM sequential test of log-likelihood ratios $S_t = \max(0, S_{t-1} + \ln(f_1(y_t)/f_0(y_t)))$ to detect persistent non-stationary structural drift breaks. Mean-reversion entries are conditionally gated: execution is suspended when the CUSUM drift statistic exceeds barrier $h$, preventing catastrophic counter-trend option theta bleed during macro regime shifts.

Dossier Edge Playbook
Audit Ledger • Post-Mortem Diagnostics

What This Module Does
A complete trade diary tracking every simulated ATM Call and Put trade from entry to exit. It factors in real-world option prices, daily theta decay, and volatility swings so you see realistic dollar gains and losses. If a trade lost money, it gives you a straight-talk post-mortem diagnosis of why (e.g. killed by theta bleed or the stock kept trending against you).
Maintains a chronological, trade-by-trade audit journal of simulated ATM Calls and Puts ($K = \text{round}(S_{\text{entry}})$) dynamically filtered by Barrier σ, Tenor, and Holding Days. Models exact Black-Scholes option pricing with 20D Realized Volatility and non-linear theta decay.
How to Profit & Trade It
Review your scorecard before putting real money on the line. Look at the Win Rate % and Total Dollar P&L. Then look down the list at the losing trades: if the Failure Cause says "Theta Bleed", holding that long ate up option value, so shorten your holding days or pick a longer expiration date next time.
Provides complete option attribution: $\Delta V = \Delta \cdot \Delta S + \frac{1}{2}\Gamma(\Delta S)^2 + \Theta \cdot \Delta t + \nu \cdot \Delta \sigma_{20\text{D}}$. Each simulated execution parses path-dependent theta burn against drift velocity. Unprofitable trajectories are algorithmically classified by root-cause failure vectors (Theta Decay $>60\%$ of loss vs Structural Drift Continuation) for empirical hyperparameter re-calibration.
